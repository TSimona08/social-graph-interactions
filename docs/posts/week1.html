The real surprise: the category isn't stable It grew by 60 pages in under a month, plus 10 subcategories whose members never counted as "direct." That's almost certainly why live Spider-Man in-degree roughly doubled — more members exist to do the linking. Exactly why the course freezes one snapshot: without it, eight groups computing "the same" network would each see a different, silently drifting graph.
The one gotcha The edge file's header row is itself comment-prefixed (# source target) — a naive comment="#" read eats it along with the real comments. Skip the leading # block by hand and name columns explicitly instead. Full script: analysis/week1_analysis.py.
