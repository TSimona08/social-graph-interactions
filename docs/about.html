About this site

A weekly interactive data story for 02805 Social Graphs and Interactions, DTU.

This site is our lab notebook for the second half of 02805: a shared playground of 303 characters from Wikipedia's Category:Marvel Comics superheroes, linked to each other whenever one character's article links to another's. Every week the course hands out a new tool or question, and every week we add one post — a small, self-contained "interactive data story" about something we found (or didn't).

The dataset is frozen at the start of the course so every group works from identical data, but our posts are free to reach past the snapshot — including re-crawling pieces of it ourselves with the Wikipedia API to sanity-check what we're told.

Tools

Python (NetworkX, pandas, matplotlib), the Wikipedia API, and an LLM co-pilot for scaffolding, styling, and keeping the write-ups honest.
